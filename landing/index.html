How it works

Like a car seat, but for software.

The manufacturer builds the engine. The seat and mirrors adjust to whoever is driving. Morph does the same for your UI.

01

Register your components

Point Morph at the components you already ship and the default layout you already designed. No rewrite.

02

Users ask for what they want

"Put my orders first." "Hide recommendations." "Make it simpler." A chat panel turns requests into a new arrangement.

03

Their layout sticks

Each layout is checked against your rules, saved per user, and follows them across sessions and devices.

Guardrails

Users rearrange. Nothing breaks.

Morph only changes how your components are arranged. It never touches what they do or your backend.

  • Your rules always winKeep checkout visible, the nav on top, legal text on screen. Layouts that break a rule are fixed or rejected.
  • Checked on screenRules are verified against the rendered page, so "visible" really means visible.
  • Undo anythingEvery layout is versioned. Users can step back or reset to your default at any time.
// morph.rules.ts
export const rules = [
  {
    component: "CreateInvoice",
    mustBe: ["visible", "aboveFold"],
  },
  {
    component: "Nav",
    mustBe: ["pinnedTop"],
  },
  {
    component: "BillingNotice",
    mustBe: ["present"],
  },
];
